Arcade: beat 8 fighters in a row for a high score. The first two go easy on you; a continue costs 20% of your score.

How to play

Touch

  • Left thumb slide to move
  • Flick up jump
  • Pull down block
  • PUNCH keep tapping for a combo
  • Down + PUNCH uppercut
  • KICK big kick
  • SUPER tap when it glows

Keyboard

  • AD / ←→ move
  • W / Space jump
  • S / ↓ hold to block
  • J jab   K punch   L hook
  • I uppercut   U kick
  • O special (½ power)   P super special (full)
  • Esc pause

Gamepad

  • Stick / D-pad move, up jumps, down blocks
  • X punch (tap again to combo)   Y uppercut   B kick   A jump
  • LB special   RB super   Start pause
  • In menus: stick to choose, A to press, B to go back

Tips

  • Chain hits: jab → punch → hook → uppercut. Press the next one as a hit lands.
  • Landing hits and taking damage fills POWER. Half bar: special move. Full bar: super special. Every fighter has their own.
  • Best of three rounds: two K.O.s wins. Power carries over between rounds.
  • Each round lasts 99 seconds. If time runs out, whoever has more health wins it.
  • Arcade: beat 8 fighters in a row. Score comes from damage, combos, and the health and time you have left; a full-health round is a PERFECT. Harder levels score more. A continue costs 20% of your score. Clear it and your fighter gets a star.
  • Hit someone mid-attack for a COUNTER (bonus damage).
  • Attack while jumping for a flying kick.
  • Turn your phone sideways to play.
